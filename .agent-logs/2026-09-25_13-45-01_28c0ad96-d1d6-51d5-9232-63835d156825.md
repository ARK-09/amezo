**So why was there a bug at all?** Because react-router can't make this call for you. It has no way to know whether a given URL change is *a navigation* (something you'd expect Back to undo) or *an edit to the view you're already on*. Both are "the URL changed". | Search box (`patchTerm`) | replace after the 1st char | keystrokes aren't navigations |
